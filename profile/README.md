
### 💻 About Us

Our **ultimate goal** is to complete **self-bank**—an ERC-4337-based Web3 sovereign banking protocol.

As we progress toward the final product, modules capable of standing independently naturally emerge at various stages. We have chosen to open-source 
these modules, serving both as a record of the development process and as a contribution of reusable tools and knowledge to the community.

---

### 🌱 Philosophy

We do not view intermediate products as "by-products," but rather as **independently verifiable, reusable building blocks**.

*   **[ip-web](https://github.com/JaiMoDAO/ip-web)** - Official IP Identity and ENS Domain Verification.
*   **[test-ApeWorX](https://github.com/JaiMoDAO/test-ApeWorX)** - A local testing environment for Solidity on-chain contract interactions.
*   Every genuine need encountered could become the next open-source module.
